# Cloud Native Summit Munich CFP Notes

## Core Guidance

Thanks for your interest in submitting for this special Cloud Native Summit event.

First-time speakers are encouraged to submit. You do not need to be a chief architect or long-time industry pundit to propose a session.

## As You Write the Proposal

- Explain what the audience will gain from the presentation.
- Explain why you are the right person to give this talk.
- Tell your unique story.
- Show how the talk fits the Kubernetes community and the broader open source ecosystem.

## Avoid

- Sales or marketing pitches
- Unlicensed or potentially closed-source technologies

The talk should focus on ideas, experiences, and open technologies rather than company promotion.

## Speaker Compensation and Accommodation

As an open source community-driven event, the summit cannot provide compensation or accommodation for speakers.

## AI Usage Policy

Avoid low-effort AI-generated proposals. AI may be used as a helper, especially for non-native English speakers, but submissions should feel thoughtful, specific, and genuinely authored.

## Code of Conduct

Submitting means agreeing to the CNS Code of Conduct.

## Commitment to Inclusivity

Review the CNS Diversity and Inclusion commitment.

## Contact

Questions about the proposal process can be sent to `team@cloudnativesummit.de`.
